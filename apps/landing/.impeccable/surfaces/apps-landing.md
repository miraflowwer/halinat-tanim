# Surface brief: landing cover (persuade)

Scope: apps/landing cover page only. Mode: persuade.
Audience: first-time visitors, farmers and cooperatives, varied literacy; EN A2 + natural Tagalog.
Job: decide to start. One promise, three ledger facts, two signing actions.
Proof/content: tagline + intro state the pre-planting comparison; ledger lines state risk answers with inputs, Luzon-only coverage, labeled demo data; actions route to auth privacy/login and docs. No fabricated claims.
Constraints: keep shared EN-TL keys, cross-app URLs from config, language persistence; calm public-service; light ground for outdoor glare; no new deps.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A logbook cover, not a marketing page. Masthead, ruled facts, and signing actions.

OWN-WORLD: The Farm Logbook System. Paper ground, ink-green type, ledger-list facts with ruled lines, cover title as the display voice. No kickers, no metric cards, no ghost cards.

STORY: A farmer hears about TANIM at the cooperative hall and opens the cover on a phone. In one screen: what it does, where it works, and where to sign.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Brand header with language control, cover tagline large, intro, three ruled ledger facts, create-account and log-in actions, help-pages link.

FORM: Shared foundation stack, cover-title display scale, ledger-list rules, button-row actions, themed selection/caret/focus. Seed key: 995a9d63.
