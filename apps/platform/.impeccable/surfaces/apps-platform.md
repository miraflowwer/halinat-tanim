# Surface brief: platform core (operate)

Scope: apps/platform core planting flow + phase6 utilities (crops, supply map, prices, suitability, weather). Mode: operate.
Audience: smallholder farmers pre-planting on phones, cooperatives coordinating; varied literacy; EN A2 + natural Tagalog.
Job: compare planned crop supply with reference level before planting decisions are finalized. Coordination, not market promise.
Proof/content: collective plans visible; deterministic Low/Moderate/High Glut Risk with inputs shown; Luzon-only Region/Province/Municipality-City; labeled synthetic data; split consent.
Constraints: keep flows, copy, EN-TL keys, API wiring; visual system only; calm public-service, never SaaS dashboard; light ground for outdoor glare; no new deps.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A field logbook, not a dashboard. Ruled paper, ledger lines, and stamped risk marks make every number feel written down and accountable.

OWN-WORLD: The Farm Logbook System. Paper-ground pages with faint rule lines, ink-green type, ledger-row facts instead of metric cards, and a three-ink risk stamp (low/moderate/high) with plain labels. No kickers, no left-border callouts, no ghost cards.

STORY: Thursday morning, Nang Rosa checks the barangay-shared plan beside her own seed list. The logbook answers in one glance: what we planted together, what the reference says, and what to do next.

FIRST VIEWPORT: HomeView greeting as a logbook page header, then collective plan as ruled ledger rows, then the risk stamp large and plain with inputs shown. One authored moment: the stamp lands with a soft exponential settle.

FORM: System workhorse stack, tabular numerals for data, 12-16px card radii with border-only elevation, pill status labels, themed selection/caret/focus/scrollbars. Seed key: 995a9d63.
